Our story

Relief. Recovery. Balance.

At Verta Fit, we believe everyday relief shouldn't be complicated — or expensive. We design and curate recovery and wellness products that help real people move better, sit better, and feel better, one small habit at a time.

Why we started

Built for the hours after.

Most of us spend our days hunched over desks, phones and steering wheels — and our bodies pay for it. Stiff necks, tight shoulders and aching lower backs have become the norm instead of the exception. Verta Fit was built to change that.

We didn't want to sell one-size-fits-all gadgets that end up in a drawer after a week. So every product we offer is chosen and refined with one question in mind: will this actually make someone's day-to-day life more comfortable?
