## Type
- Display: **Barlow Condensed** 700, uppercase (headlines, prices, stats)
- Body: **Barlow** 400/600 (paragraphs, nav, labels)

## Signature
Recovery pulse — an ember EKG waveform with a soft glow. Appears in the logo mark,
the hero canvas, and the product spotlight rings. Ember is spent only on the pulse
and CTAs; everything else stays dark and quiet.
